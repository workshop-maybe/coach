Andamio Desktop helps you find out which of your AI agents can do the work you care about. A first run, from here to your first recorded test, takes about 30 minutes.

The app is an early preview, and some things will break. When one does, or something confuses you, [send feedback](/help/feedback/).

## What you need

- **The build James sends you.** The app runs on Mac and Linux.
- **An AI assistant you already use**, such as Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor. Anything you can paste a prompt into will do. The first test works best in an assistant that is open in a repo you know, such as Claude Code or Cursor.

## Install it

1. Open the file James sends you and install Andamio Desktop as you would any other app.
2. Open Andamio Desktop.

The window has its own close, minimize and full-screen keys at the top left, and you resize it from its edges.

## If your Mac won't open it

The first time you open the app, your Mac may say it can't open it. If it does:

1. Open **System Settings**, then **Privacy & Security**.
2. Scroll down to the line about Andamio Desktop and click **Open Anyway**.
3. Confirm, then open the app again.

You only need to do this once.

## What stays on your computer

- Everything you make stays in a folder you choose. There is no account.
- The app never talks to your assistant. You copy prompts out and paste replies back in.
- Nothing leaves your computer unless you turn on [Jev Mode](/help/jev-mode/), which is off by default. Jev Mode uses your own TypeSafe key and sends text to TypeSafe only when you ask.
