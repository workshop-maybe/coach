The app keeps everything you make in one folder on your computer. Before you open it the first time, make an empty folder somewhere easy to find, such as `Documents/Andamio`.

## Pick it

The first time the app opens, it asks for a folder.

<Shot id="first-run" alt="The first screen: the Andamio logo, a line asking for a folder for your credentials, agents and results, and the Choose folder button" />

1. Click <UI>Choose folder</UI>.
2. Pick the empty folder you made.

The app remembers it. Next time, it opens straight to home.

## What goes in it

Your credentials, your agents and every result you record go in this folder as plain text: Markdown and JSON files you can open and read. The folder is the whole record. You can back it up, or put it under git. [Your data](/help/your-data/) shows how it's laid out, and the little the app keeps outside it.

The folder's path shows in the top bar on home. To work from another folder, click <UI>Change folder</UI>.
