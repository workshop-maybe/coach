The app keeps everything you make in one folder on your computer. Before you open it the first time, make an empty folder somewhere easy to find, such as `Documents/Coach`.

## Pick it

The first time the app opens, it plays [the intro](/getting-started/intro/), then asks for a folder. If you wrote a line at the end of the intro, the app says your badge will be saved in the folder you pick. If you skipped, it asks for a folder for your credentials, agents and results.

<Shot id="first-run" alt="The folder screen after the intro: the Coach logo, a line saying your badge is saved in the folder you pick with your agents and results, and the Choose folder button" />

1. Click <UI>Choose folder</UI>.
2. Pick the empty folder you made.

The app remembers it. Next time, it opens straight to home, with no intro. If you wrote a line, home opens with your new badge on it: [Run your first test](/getting-started/first-test/) goes on from there.

## What goes in it

Your credentials, your agents and every result you record go in this folder as plain text: Markdown and JSON files you can open and read. The folder is the whole record. You can back it up, or put it under git. [Your data](/help/your-data/) shows how it's laid out, and the little the app keeps outside it.

The folder's path shows in the top bar on home. To work from another folder, click <UI>Change folder</UI>.

If you move or rename the folder while the app is closed, it asks for it again next time, without the intro: [When something breaks](/help/when-something-breaks/#your-folder-moved-or-was-renamed).
