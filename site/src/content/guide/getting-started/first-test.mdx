A test hands one badge's task to one agent. You copy the prompt into your assistant, paste its answer back into the app, and decide whether it met the badge's bar. Your first test runs on the badge your line made at the end of [the intro](/getting-started/intro/). If you skipped that, the app makes a badge for you.

## Give your badge a task

Your line says what good looks like. The badge still needs a task: what the agent is asked to do. Home opens with your badge under <UI>Badges</UI> and its row lit under <UI>What needs you</UI>: <UI>Finish \{name\}</UI>, <UI>Add its task</UI>.

<Shot id="first-badge-home" alt="Home after the first run: the lit row Finish Every fix ships with a test, Add its task, under What needs you, and the Every fix ships with a test badge under Badges with no holders yet" />

1. Click the row. <UI>The task</UI> opens.
2. Write the task yourself, or let your agent draft it: click <UI>Suggest a task</UI>, paste the prompt into your assistant, and paste the task it suggests back into the box. Change it until it asks for what you meant.
3. Click <UI>Save</UI>. The badge opens. Close it to go home.

<Shot id="first-badge-task" alt="The task card for the new badge: an empty task box, the Suggest a task, Review this assignment and Write a refine prompt links below it, and Save" />

## Start it

With a task on your badge and no agents yet, home leads with <UI>Try a first test</UI>. Click it. The test runs on your badge.

If you skipped the intro's line, home is empty instead. <UI>Try a first test</UI> runs on a badge the app makes for you, *Name what matters next*, and the pictures on the rest of this page show that one.

<Shot id="home-empty" alt="Home in an empty folder: the Try a first test button above the three doors, Create an agent, Create a credential and Find an agent for a job, and Watch the intro at the top right" />

If you have badges but none has a task yet, there's no <UI>Try a first test</UI>. Add a task to one first, as above.

## Set up the agent

An agent is the assistant you'll test, as you run it: its harness, its model and its effort.

1. Turn the <UI>Harness</UI>, <UI>Model</UI> and <UI>Effort</UI> dials to match the assistant you use. Click the arrows on either side of a dial, or click the dial and use the arrow keys.
2. If yours isn't listed, turn the dial to <UI>Other</UI> and type its name.
3. Some models also have a <UI>Version</UI> dial. Leave it on <UI>latest</UI> unless you use an older version.
4. Click <UI>Make the agent</UI>.

<Shot id="first-test-agent" alt="The agent card: the agent drawn above its Harness, Model, Version and Effort dials, set to Claude Code, opus, latest and high, with Make the agent below" />

The agent is named for its setup, such as *Claude Code, opus, high*. You can rename it later.

On your own badge, the test goes straight on to handing it over. If you skipped the intro's line, the app also makes *Name what matters next* now. Its task asks an agent open in a repo to name the three things most worth doing next, with evidence for each. Later you can [build more badges](/how-to/build-a-credential/), with as many learning targets as each needs.

## Hand it over

<Shot id="first-test-hand" alt="Hand it over: the badge in a box beside the agent, the S · default handoff key lit, and the Copy button" />

1. Click <UI>Copy</UI>. The handoff, the prompt that gives the badge's task to the agent, is now on your clipboard.
2. Paste it into your assistant and let it work. It does best open in a repo you know.
3. Wait for its whole answer.

## Bring back the reply

1. Copy your assistant's whole answer.
2. Paste it into the box under <UI>What came back</UI>. If it made files, such as a PDF, add them with <UI>File</UI> under the box.
3. Click <UI>Next</UI>.

If you need to stop before you have a reply, close the test. The agent keeps the handoff, and home shows <UI>Pull \{agent\}'s handoff</UI> under <UI>What needs you</UI>. Click it to pick up where you left off.

## Judge it

<Shot id="first-test-judge" alt="Did it meet it: the badge's rubric on the left, the pasted reply on the right, and the Met, Not yet and Hold to compare buttons below" />

<UI>Did it meet it</UI> shows the badge's rubric beside the reply. The badge from your line has no rubric yet, so it shows the reply alone: judge it against your line. You can [add a rubric](/how-to/change-a-badge/) later.

1. Read the rubric. With no rubric, keep your line in mind: it's the bar.
2. Check the reply against it. The rubric of *Name what matters next* asks you to look up every file, commit and check the reply names. Only you can judge this one, because you know the repo.
3. Choose <UI>Met</UI> or <UI>Not yet</UI>. <UI>Hold to compare</UI> is for later, when you want to read several agents' replies side by side before you judge: [Run a bake-off](/how-to/run-a-bake-off/).

The app records your verdict with the reply. On <UI>Met</UI>, it shows <UI>\{agent\} holds it</UI> and waits. Click <UI>Make \{agent\} the go-to</UI> to choose this agent for the badge, which takes you home. [Choose a go-to](/how-to/choose-a-go-to/) explains it. On <UI>Not yet</UI>, a badge with a rubric asks <UI>Which points did it miss</UI>. Tap the rubric points the reply missed, or none, and click <UI>Save</UI>. Then click <UI>Done</UI> to go home, or <UI>Try another agent</UI> to hand the same handoff to another agent.

## Look at home

<Shot id="first-test-home" alt="Home after the first test: the agent under Agents, and the Name what matters next badge under Badges with the agent shown as its holder and named as its go-to" />

Your agent and your badge are both on home now. If you chose <UI>Met</UI>, the badge shows your agent as its holder, and your agent lights a key for the badge. If you made it the go-to, the badge names it: <UI>Go-to: \{agent\}</UI>.

That's a whole test. [Ideas](/ideas/credentials/) explains what credentials, agents and judging mean in this app. Tell James how it went: [send feedback](/help/feedback/).
