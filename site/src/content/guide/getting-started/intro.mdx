The first time you open the app, it plays a short intro before it asks for a folder. It takes about 40 seconds and ends by asking you for one line: what a good result looks like for something you hand to an agent. That line becomes your first badge.

## Start it

The intro opens on a title card.

<Shot id="intro-title" alt="The title card: the Andamio logo on the board, with the Begin and Skip buttons below it" />

- Click <UI>Begin</UI> to play it. It has sound.
- Click <UI>Skip</UI>, or press Escape, to go straight to [choosing your folder](/getting-started/your-folder/).

While it plays, <UI>Skip</UI> stays at the top right, and Escape still skips. <UI>Mute</UI> beside it turns the sound off, and <UI>Unmute</UI> turns it back on. The M key does the same. The app remembers your choice for next time.

## If your system reduces motion

When your computer is set to reduce motion, nothing in the intro moves and it plays no sound. Each scene shows as a still picture, and you move on when you're ready: click <UI>Next</UI>, or press Enter, Space or the right arrow. <UI>Skip</UI> and Escape work as before. There is no <UI>Mute</UI>, since there is nothing to mute.

<Shot id="intro-still" alt="A still from the intro: the line Slow down. Say what good looks like., a credential drawn as a cartridge reading every claim cites the line it came from, an agent beside it, and the Next and Skip buttons at the top right" />

## Say what good looks like

When the film ends, a card asks <UI>What would a good result look like</UI>.

<Shot id="intro-play" alt="The card What would a good result look like over the intro's last scene, with the line Every fix ships with a test typed in and the Make it a badge button" />

1. Think of one thing you hand to an agent, such as fixing a bug or writing a release note.
2. Write what it has to do for you to call it good, in one line. For example: *Every fix ships with a test*.
3. Click <UI>Make it a badge</UI>.

The line becomes your first badge's learning target, and the badge is named from its first few words. The app saves it once you've [chosen your folder](/getting-started/your-folder/), then [Run your first test](/getting-started/first-test/) picks it up from there.

If you'd rather not write one now, close the card with its × or <UI>Back</UI>. Nothing is saved, and you go on to choose your folder.

## Watch it again

Click <UI>Watch the intro</UI> at the top right of home. It plays the same film with the same keys, and leaves out the card that asks for a line. When it ends, or when you skip it, you're back on home.
