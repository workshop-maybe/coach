Export writes your credentials out as Markdown and JSON files, in a folder you choose. The files follow the layout the Andamio command-line tool imports.

## Export your credentials

1. On home, click <UI>Export</UI> in the top bar. It stays greyed out until your folder has a credential.
2. Your system's folder picker opens. Choose the folder to export into, or cancel to export nothing.
3. The app writes the files. A line under the top bar says how many files it wrote and where. <UI>Dismiss</UI> closes it.

Exporting again to the same folder replaces the files that have the same names.

## What goes out

Your credentials sit in a collection, a group with its own name. Most folders have one, `my-credentials`. For each collection, the export writes a folder with one numbered folder per credential, and one JSON file:

```text
my-credentials/
  1/
    outline.md
    assignment.md
    badge.svg
  2/
    …
my-credentials.json
```

- `outline.md` holds the credential's name and its learning targets.
- `assignment.md` holds its name and its task.
- `badge.svg` is its badge, in the colors, art and title you gave it.
- The JSON file holds every credential in the collection, with the same names, learning targets and tasks.

Each credential keeps its number from one export to the next.

## What stays out

- **The rubric.** No exported file holds it. It stays in your folder, so the bar you judge by stays yours.
- **Your agents, results and handoffs.** Export covers credentials only. Everything else is already plain text in your folder, as [Your data](/help/your-data/) shows.

If a badge's art can't be read, the export writes that badge without its art and the line under the top bar says so. If a collection's file can't be read, the export skips that collection and names it.
