Every result keeps the reply that earned it. A badge's page lists them all, so you can read what each agent sent back and compare any two. Each reply is a take: one agent's try at the badge.

## Get two takes

You need two replies to the same badge.

1. Make a second agent with a different model or effort. See [Make an agent](/how-to/make-an-agent/).
2. Give it the same badge. See [Hand off a test](/how-to/hand-off-a-test/).

Two takes by the same agent compare too. Under an agent's reply on the badge's page, <UI>Test again</UI> starts another test for it.

## Find the replies

Open the badge from home. Under <UI>Replies</UI>, beside its parts, each agent that took the test has a row with its verdict, the date, the computer it ran on and the start of its reply. An agent's latest take leads its row, with any older takes below.

<Shot id="badge-replies" alt="Say how sure you are's page: its parts on the left, and on the right its Replies, Claude Code, Met, and Codex, Not met, each with the start of its reply and both ticked, the Compare button above them, and Claude Code under Holders, marked Go-to" />

Once two or more of the badge's handoffs have results, <UI>Replies</UI> lists each handoff that ran instead, with how many agents ran it and how many met. Open one to see its replies, laid out the same way.

## Read one in full

Click an agent's name or its reply to open the take.

<Shot id="reply-view" alt="Codex's take on Say how sure you are: a bar with codex · gpt-6-astra · medium, Not met and Sep 25, 2026, a note on the result, then the reply in full, and the Skeleton handoff it ran, folded, below" />

The bar at the top shows the agent, its setup, the verdict and the date. <UI>Show as stored</UI> shows the reply as Markdown. Below the reply, the handoff it ran is folded under its name, such as <UI>Skeleton</UI>. Click the name to read the exact text the agent got. A take judged with Jev's help also keeps Jev's reading there.

Close the take with the × or Escape. You're back on the badge, with that take's row lit.

## Put two side by side

1. Tick the boxes beside two takes. Until you have two, the button reads <UI>Pick two to compare</UI>. A third tick drops the first one you ticked.
2. Click <UI>Compare</UI>.

<Shot id="compare" alt="Two takes side by side: Codex, Not met, on the left and Claude Code, Met, on the right, each with its setup and date above its reply, and Show as stored above both" />

Each reply scrolls on its own. One <UI>Show as stored</UI> switch changes both. Close the compare, and you're back on the badge with both takes still ticked.

Once you know which one you'll use, make it the badge's go-to: [Choose a go-to](/how-to/choose-a-go-to/).
