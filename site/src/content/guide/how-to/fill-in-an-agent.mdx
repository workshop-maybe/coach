Open an agent from home. Its card shows its dials. Beside the card are its parts and the badges it has tried, or below it on a narrow window.

<Shot id="agent-page" alt="Claude Code's page: its card with locked dials and Make a new agent like this, and beside it the part cards Name, Repo, Notes and Parameters, the two badges it holds under Badges, and Move to trash" />

## Its parts

Click a part to change it, then click <UI>Save</UI>.

- <UI>Name</UI>: what you call it, under <UI>What is it called</UI>.
- <UI>Repo</UI>: <UI>Where it works</UI>, the folder it opens in.
- <UI>Notes</UI>: anything worth remembering about it, such as what it's good at.
- <UI>Parameters</UI>: any other settings it runs with, one per line, as `name = value`. Type one and press Enter, or click <UI>Add another</UI>. Remove one with its ×.

<Shot id="agent-params" alt="The Parameters card: the line max turns = 40 above an empty name = value field, Add another, and the Save button" />

## Its setup

Until the agent has a result, you can turn its dials here and click <UI>Keep this setup</UI>. Once it has one, the dials lock, so every result stays tied to the setup that produced it. To try another model or effort, click <UI>Make a new agent like this</UI>. [Make an agent](/how-to/make-an-agent/) has the steps.

## Its badges

Under <UI>Badges</UI> is every badge the agent has tried. Click one to open the reply that counts.

- A badge it hasn't met says <UI>Not met</UI>.
- A badge it holds whose task or rubric changed since says <UI>Tested against an earlier version of the assignment</UI>.
- A badge it holds from before its setup changed says <UI>Agent setup changed since this result</UI>.

An agent with no results says <UI>Not tested yet.</UI> Click <UI>Test it</UI> to start one.

The keys on the agent's drawing light up for the badges it holds. Click a lit key to open that badge's reply.

If the agent holds a handoff, <UI>Pull the handoff</UI> above its parts opens that test at <UI>What came back</UI>.

## Move it to the trash

Below the agent's badges, click <UI>Move to trash</UI>.

<Shot id="trash-agent" alt="Move Codex to trash: Its 2 results go with it. Its handoff is pulled with no result. With Back and the Move to trash button" />

The card says what goes with the agent: its results, and the handoff it holds, which is pulled with no result. Click <UI>Move to trash</UI>. A badge that had it as its go-to has none until you move its files back.

Nothing is deleted. The files move into a `trash` folder inside your folder, so you can get them back.
