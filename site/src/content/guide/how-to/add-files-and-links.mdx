Agents often hand back more than text: a report as a PDF, a file they wrote, a screen recording, a link to a pull request. A reply can hold all of these. You add them on <UI>What came back</UI>, and they show in place wherever the reply does.

## Add to the reply

The reply starts as one text box, <UI>Reply</UI>. Paste the agent's answer there as usual. Under the box are five keys:

- <UI>Text</UI> adds another text box.
- <UI>Image</UI>, <UI>Video</UI> and <UI>File</UI> open a picker. You can choose several files at once. While they're copied, the reply shows <UI>Adding</UI>.
- <UI>Link</UI> opens a line for an address, such as `https://`. Type or paste it, then press Enter or click away. Only http, https and mailto addresses go in; anything else shows <UI>Only http, https and mailto links can be added</UI>.

Each part goes at the end, in the order you add it. The app sorts files by what they are, not by the key you used: a PNG or JPEG becomes a picture, an MP4, MOV, M4V or WebM a video, and anything else a file.

You can also:

- Drag files from Finder, or your file manager, onto the reply. Each one becomes a part.
- Copy a PDF or another file in Finder and paste it into the text. It becomes a part. A picture or a video you paste into the text stays in the text, as in [Paste an image](/how-to/paste-an-image/) and [Add a video](/how-to/add-a-video/).

Once the reply has more than one part, each part has a bar naming it and a <UI>Remove</UI> key. Nothing is recorded until you click <UI>Met</UI>, <UI>Not yet</UI> or <UI>Hold to compare</UI>, and <UI>Next</UI> waits until the reply has something in it: text, a file or a link.

## Judge it in place

<Shot id="reply-items" alt="Did it meet it: the Write a release note rubric on the left, and on the right the reply as a stack: a Text part with Claude Code's line about the note, a Link part with the pull request's address, and a File part, release-notes-0.9.pdf, showing its first page" />

On <UI>Did it meet it</UI>, on the reply's own page, on a held reply and in a compare, every part shows where it sits in the reply:

- Text shows as it always has.
- A picture shows at a readable size, and a video plays.
- A PDF shows page by page. If the app can't read it, it says <UI>Can't show this PDF</UI> and offers <UI>Open</UI>.
- A Markdown or text file (`.md`, `.markdown` or `.txt`) shows its contents. One over 1 MB shows its name and <UI>Open</UI>.
- A link shows its address and opens in your browser.
- Any other file shows its name and <UI>Open</UI>. <UI>Open</UI> opens documents, pictures and archives, such as a Word file or a ZIP, in their usual app. For any other kind of file, it shows the file in its folder instead, so nothing runs by accident.

A part whose file is no longer in your folder shows its name and says it is missing.

In a compare, each reply is its own stack and scrolls on its own, so you can read one agent's PDF beside another's.

## Where the files go

The app copies each file into your folder, so the reply keeps it after the agent's copy is gone. A file or a video can be up to 100 MB, and a picture up to 10 MB. Pictures and videos sit beside the result, and other files in a `files/` folder there. The reply keeps each file's own name, while the copy in your folder gets a new one. [Your data](/help/your-data/) shows the folders.

The file is copied the moment you add it. If you remove it, or close the test without recording, the copy stays in your folder.

## What Jev reads

With [Jev Mode](/help/jev-mode/) on, Jev reads the reply's text and the contents of its Markdown and text files. It can't see pictures, videos, PDFs or other files, and it can't open links. It is told each one is there, by name or address, and asked not to count a point either way when its evidence would sit only there, so such a point usually reads as Can't call. Judge that point yourself: the pictures, videos and files you add are here so you can see what the agent made with your own eyes. Beside <UI>Ask Jev</UI>, the judge pane says so: "Jev reads the words. The pictures, videos, files and links are here for your own eyes." [The second opinion, end to end](/jev/second-opinion/#a-reply-with-files-and-links) shows exactly what goes.
