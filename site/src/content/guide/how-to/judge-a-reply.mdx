After you bring back a reply and click <UI>Next</UI>, the test asks <UI>Did it meet it</UI>. The badge's rubric is on the left and the reply is on the right.

<Shot id="judge" alt="Did it meet it: the Write a release note rubric on the left, Claude Code's release note on the right, and the Met and Not yet buttons below" />

## Read it against the rubric

1. Read the rubric one point at a time.
2. For each point, find where the reply meets it or misses it.
3. Check what the reply cites. Look up the files, commits and facts it names. One invented piece of evidence is enough to fail it.

<UI>Show as stored</UI> shows the rubric and the reply as the Markdown they're stored as. <UI>Show formatted</UI> goes back.

Pasted the wrong thing? <UI>Back</UI> returns to the reply box with your paste still in it.

A badge with no rubric shows only the reply. Give it one before you rely on its results: [Change a badge](/how-to/change-a-badge/).

## Record your verdict

- Click <UI>Met</UI> when the reply meets the rubric as written. The app shows <UI>\{agent\} holds it</UI> and waits. Click <UI>Make \{agent\} the go-to</UI> to choose this agent for the badge, or <UI>Home</UI> to leave it. [Choose a go-to](/how-to/choose-a-go-to/) explains the choice.
- Click <UI>Not yet</UI> when it doesn't. The card says <UI>\{agent\} does not hold it</UI>. Click <UI>Done</UI> to go home, or <UI>Try another agent</UI> to give the same badge to another of your agents.

Either way, the app saves a result in your folder: your verdict, the reply as it stands in the reply box, and the handoff you copied. The badge's page lists the reply under <UI>Replies</UI>, and on <UI>Met</UI> the agent lights a key for the badge on home.

A result is never overwritten. To judge an agent again, test it again. Its newest result is the one that counts, and the older ones stay on the badge's page. [Compare takes](/how-to/compare-takes/) shows how to read them.

## A second opinion

With [Jev Mode](/help/jev-mode/) on, the card can also offer <UI>Ask Jev</UI>, and <UI>Met</UI> and <UI>Not yet</UI> become choices you confirm with <UI>Save</UI>. Jev Mode is off by default, and you can judge every reply without it. [Judging a result](/ideas/judging/) explains why the call is always yours.
