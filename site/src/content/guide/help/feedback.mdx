Coach is an early preview. James sends builds to a small group of testers, and things will break. Telling James when they do helps, and so does telling him what worked. The badge drawings are placeholders, so tell him about the words and the flow before the pictures.

Send your notes to James at [james@andamio.io](mailto:james@andamio.io).

## How to send it

Short notes are perfect. Send them however is easiest: an email, a voice memo or a few screenshots. A screen recording where you talk through what you're doing is the most useful of all.

## What to tell James

- Where did you get stuck, or have to guess what to do next?
- Which words didn't make sense?
- What felt broken, slow or ugly?
- What did you enjoy?
- Would you use it again? For what?

## When something breaks

Say what you did, what you expected and what happened, and add a screenshot if you can. [When something breaks](/help/when-something-breaks/) may already have the fix.

Never send your TypeSafe key. Your folder is plain text, so before you attach a file from it, check that you're happy for James to read what's in it.
